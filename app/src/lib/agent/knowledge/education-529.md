# Education savings (529 plans)

If there are kids and college is a goal, save for it in a dedicated 529 plan.

- **Use a 529 plan** — contributions grow tax-free and are tax-free when used
  for qualified education expenses.
- **Keep it separate.** The 529 is its own account and its own goal — don't
  commingle it with retirement or general investments, so each goal is tracked
  and funded independently.
- **Start early.** Contributing while the child is young gives the balance the
  most time to compound before tuition is due.
- **One per child / goal** as appropriate, so each is sized to its own timeline.

General education, not personalized or tax advice.
