# Saving for large near-term purchases

For a big, planned purchase in the next few years — a house down payment, a car,
similar — the money shouldn't ride the stock market's volatility, but it also
shouldn't sit idle.

- **Keep it conservative but earning.** Use **money market funds** or **CDs**
  (certificates of deposit) — safe, and still earning a real yield.
- **Match the term to the timeline.** For money needed at a known date, a CD
  ladder or a CD maturing near that date can lock in a rate; a money market fund
  keeps it fully liquid if the timing is flexible.
- **Don't invest it in stocks/bonds.** The goal here is capital preservation for
  a near-term need, not long-term growth — a market drop right before the
  purchase would be costly.
- **Separate from the emergency fund and the long-term portfolio** so each pool
  is sized and managed to its own purpose.

General education, not personalized advice.
