# High-interest debt

Avoid high-interest debt, and if it exists, pay it off before investing beyond
any employer match.

- **What counts:** credit-card balances, personal loans, and similar — anything
  in the ~10%+ range, and especially 20%+.
- **Why first:** paying off a balance is a guaranteed, risk-free return equal to
  its interest rate. A 20% APR card paid down "returns" 20% — hard to beat with
  investing.
- **Approach:** prioritize the highest-rate balances first (avalanche). Keep
  making any 401(k) match contributions in parallel — the match is free money —
  but otherwise direct spare cash at the debt until it's cleared.
- **Then:** once high-interest debt is gone, move on to the emergency fund and
  investing (see `financial-priorities`).

Low-rate, tax-advantaged debt (e.g. a mortgage) is a different question and not
covered by this "pay it off first" rule.

General education, not personalized advice.
