# Emergency fund

A cash reserve for unexpected costs — job loss, illness, urgent repairs — so
you never have to sell investments or take on high-interest debt at a bad time.

- **Size:** start with about **6 months of expenses**. Adjust up or down based
  on job security, income stability, dependents, and personal risk tolerance —
  e.g. a dual-income household with stable jobs might hold less; a single earner
  in a volatile field, more.
- **Where to keep it:** a high-yield savings account or money market fund —
  liquid and safe, but still earning interest rather than sitting idle in
  checking.
- **Not invested:** the emergency fund is not part of the investment portfolio.
  Its job is stability and access, not growth, so it stays out of stocks/bonds.
- **Base it on real expenses:** size it from the user's actual monthly spending
  (available via the spending tools), not a guess.

General education, not personalized advice.
